Layering Recipe Builder

Two perfumes. One scent that is yours.

Pick two or three perfumes you already know, choose how many sprays of each, and see the blend you would get, built from their real notes.

Blends are predicted from each perfume's listed notes. They are a guide, not a lab test, so try a small amount on your skin first. Matches come from FragranceNet's catalogue. The Amazon UAE and Noon UAE buttons search for the same perfume, so check it is the right one. We may earn a commission if you buy through a link.
