Layering Recipe Builder

Combine two or three fragrances from the catalog into a saved recipe. See a predicted blend computed from each fragrance's real notes and accords, then find out how it actually wore from people who've tried the same combo.

How it'll work

Pick two or three fragrances you already know from the catalog. As each one is added, the predicted blend below updates automatically — pulled straight from the note pyramid and accords already on every product page, no new data needed. Save it, give it a name, and it becomes its own page: shareable, filterable, and open for other people to rate and leave wear notes on. This preview uses two real fragrances already on ParfAI — Baccarat Rouge 540 and Tobacco Vanille — a pairing enthusiasts already talk about, to show what the finished builder would produce.
