About ParfAI

ParfAI is a fragrance discovery platform — part deep database, part smart matching engine, part community. Our goal is simple: help you find your next signature scent, and a fair-priced way to try it.

What we do

We catalog fragrances with real notes, accords, and community ratings, then use that data to power tools like Find My Scent and the Dupe Finder — which matches expensive fragrances against affordable alternatives sharing the same accord profile.

How we make money

ParfAI is free to use. When you click through to buy a fragrance from one of our retail partners, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. See our affiliate disclosure for details.

The community

Reviews you submit through ParfAI come from real fragrance enthusiasts and are marked "NEW." The Photo wall, Scent of the Day, and some starter reviews you'll see alongside them are example content, shown while our real community grows — full community submission for all of these is next on our list.
