Best Aventus Dupes: How Close Is Club de Nuit Intense Man?

Dupes & Comparisons  ·  September 19, 2026  ·  6 min read

If you've spent any time in a fragrance forum or a "smells like" comment section, you've run into the same conversation: someone asks how to get the Creed Aventus smell without spending $395 on the real thing, and someone else answers with the same name, Armaf's Club de Nuit Intense Man. It costs around $35. We put both side by side, note for note, to see how much you're actually giving up.

That's roughly eleven times the price for the original. The question is whether the notes back that up.

What Aventus actually smells like

Aventus opens with a sparkling, almost sweet pineapple that made it famous, backed by bergamot, black currant, and apple. As it settles, it moves into birch, patchouli, Moroccan jasmine, and rose, before drying down into musk, oak moss, ambergris, and vanilla. That base is where a lot of the "expensive" feeling comes from, it's smoky and a little woody, and it lingers for 7 to 12 hours with strong projection.

How close is Club de Nuit Intense Man?

Club de Nuit Intense Man is built around the same skeleton: pineapple, blackcurrant, and apple up top, birch and jasmine in the heart, musk and ambergris underneath. If you've smelled Aventus, the opening is genuinely the part that will feel most familiar, that same fizzy, fruity brightness is there.

What's missing is the extra layering that makes Aventus feel more expensive: there's no patchouli or oak moss to give the base its smoky, mossy depth, and no rose or vanilla to round it out. The result is a simpler, more linear version of the same idea, less complex, but recognizably in the same family. Both are rated for long-lasting wear (7 to 12 hours) with strong projection, so despite being an EDT against Aventus's EDP, it doesn't fall short on staying power.

The verdict

Club de Nuit Intense Man isn't a perfect copy, and it isn't trying to be. It's a genuinely close, honest take on the opening and general shape of Aventus, at a small fraction of the price. If what you love about Aventus is that pineapple-and-smoke first impression, this gets you most of the way there. If you're someone who lives in the base notes, the extra depth from the oak moss, patchouli, and rose in the real thing is worth noticing, and worth the price to some people and not others.

Our honest take: it's one of the better starting points if you want to try the Aventus style before committing to the original, or if you just want that scent on a Tuesday without worrying about it.
