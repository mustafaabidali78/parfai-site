New Fragrance Launches Worth Knowing About, October and November 2026

New Releases  ·  November 9, 2026  ·  5 min read

October kept fragrance houses busy, and a handful of names have already confirmed what's coming in November. Here is a plain rundown of what has actually launched, and what has only been announced, as of November 9, 2026. Where a source didn't clearly state a price, a perfumer, or a date, we left it out rather than guess.

New versions of fragrances already in our catalog

Dior released its first extrait de parfum version of Sauvage, built on lavender and bergamot up top, patchouli through the middle, and vetiver, cedarwood, and oud underneath. The original Eau de Toilette Sauvage is already in our catalog if you want to see how it compares, here.

Tom Ford put out a Reserve version of Ombré Leather, listed from around $100 to $175 depending on the retailer. It opens with silver juniper and black leather, then settles into sandalwood, white moss, and violet leaf. The standard Ombré Leather, with its own different note list, is in our catalog here.

Armani released Stronger With You Spices, a limited edition priced from about $89 to $105 depending on where you buy it, built on citrus, black pepper, saffron, lavender, vanilla, and bourbon. The original Stronger With You is in our catalog here.

Montblanc gave Explorer a new Infinite Eau de Parfum in a blue bottle, priced between about $90 and $119. It opens with bergamot and lemon, moves into lavender and clary sage, and finishes on vetiver, patchouli, and musk. The original Explorer is in our catalog here.

What else launched in October

Byredo added Havana Tonka to its Night Veils collection as a 70ml perfume extract at £310, built on roasted tonka bean and bergamot, tobacco and violet in the middle, and cashmere, pink peppercorn, and rum underneath. Jo Malone released a Highlands-themed cologne called Vanilla Scotch, priced at $148.75 at Nordstrom, with vanilla, Scotch whisky, tonka bean, and amber. D.S. & Durga, a brand we don't carry yet, released Ain't That Sweet, a gourmand built on vanilla, warm spices, and malt, priced at £155 and described by the brand as inspired by date cake.

Two niche releases stood out for their detail. The Korean brand BORNTOSTANDOUT, also not yet in our catalog, added Saint Petal to its Extrait Extrême collection, composed by perfumer Jordi Fernández and priced at $320 for 50ml. It's aged three months in traditional Korean onggi pottery at a 55 to 62 percent concentration, opening with orange blossom, cumin, and bergamot, moving through violet leaves, jasmine sambac, coffee, and incense, and settling on amyris, vetiver, oud, and leather. Bvlgari released Allegra Baciami Ancora as a parfum, composed by Jacques Cavallier and priced at $385 for 100ml, centered on gardenia and vanilla.

What's arriving in November

Skylar released its Oud Butter perfume on November 1, and Orebella followed on November 3 with a Cashmere Milk parfum. Neither brand is in our catalog yet. Le Labo, which we do carry, has announced a Vienna city exclusive called Café 32, though as of this writing the brand has only confirmed the month, not an exact release date.

A pattern worth noticing

Warm, sweet bases show up again and again across this list. Tonka bean, vanilla, and amber appear in at least four of the launches above, from Byredo's Havana Tonka to Jo Malone's Vanilla Scotch to Armani's Spices. Gourmand framing, meaning food-like ideas like date cake or roasted coffee, turns up more than once too. Nine fragrances across two months isn't enough to call a trend on its own, but it fits where fall and holiday releases usually lean.

If you want to explore the notes further

Four of the launches above are new versions of fragrances already in the ParfAI catalog: Sauvage, Ombré Leather, Stronger With You, and Explorer. The newest names, like Havana Tonka, Saint Petal, and Allegra Baciami Ancora, aren't in our catalog yet since they're too new to have reviews worth tracking. You can browse Gourmand fragrances or Woody picks in our full catalog, or look up a note like tonka bean or oud on its own page to see what's already there.

How this was put together

This roundup is based on the sources listed below, covering coverage published as of November 9, 2026. We haven't tried any of these fragrances ourselves, and none of the newest launches have reached our catalog yet. Where a source gave conflicting prices across retailers, we gave a range rather than pick one.
