How this works

Your location

Found from your connection, with no pop-up. Tap Use precise location for a closer match. Only your city and today's weather are kept, in your browser.

Live weather

Temperature and conditions come from Open-Meteo, fresh for your spot. If the lookup fails, you pick a city instead.

The match

Weather sorts into hot, mild, cold, rainy or snowy. Citrus and aquatic notes suit the heat, amber and woods the cold, green notes the rain. One perfume is drawn each day.

Your units

Shown in °C or °F, set by your country.
