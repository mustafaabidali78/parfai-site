Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

This policy explains what ParfAI collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to parfai.org and any tools on it, including the Dupe Finder, Find My Scent, and the community pages.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide when you sign up, such as your name and email.
  • Content you submit, such as reviews or messages sent through our contact form.
  • Basic usage data, such as when you sign in, so we can understand how the site is used and keep it running well.

Cookies

ParfAI uses cookies to keep you signed in and to remember basic preferences. Some pages also link out to retail partners through affiliate programs. Those partners, including Amazon and others, may set their own cookies once you click through to their site, so they can credit the referral. This is standard for any site that links to online retailers, and it doesn't give us access to anything you do on their site.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and show your reviews under your name.
  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form.
  • To improve the catalog, search, and matching tools over time.
  • To understand which pages and features are actually useful, using aggregate counts only, never individual browsing profiles.

Where your information is stored

Account and submission data is stored securely with Supabase, our database provider. We don't sell your personal data to anyone, and we don't share it with third parties beyond what's needed to run the site, such as our database provider, or the standard referral data affiliate links share with retail partners.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and submissions, at any time via Contact. We'll respond as soon as we can.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, reach out through the Contact page and we'll help.
